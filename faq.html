FAQ

Questions, answered

The basics

Is it free?

Yes. There's no account, no watermark and no limit on how many videos you make.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in a web browser on a computer or phone. Chrome, Edge and Safari give the fastest, most compatible exports.

Is my recording uploaded anywhere?

No. Your voice, music and photo are processed on your device and never sent to a server. See the privacy page for details.

Do subliminals actually work?

Scientific evidence that subliminal affirmations change behaviour is limited and mixed. Many people use them like background affirmations or a calming ritual, and enjoy them on those terms.

Subliminal Studio is a creative audio and video tool. It doesn't promise results, and it isn't a substitute for medical or mental-health care.

Making a video

What files can I use for my voice?

MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, OGG, Opus, FLAC and WebM audio, or a video file with sound, such as MP4 or MOV. Your browser does the decoding, so a format that plays in your browser will almost always work.

How quiet should the voice be?

Most subliminals sit between −25 and −35 dB under the background. The guide has a table of what each level sounds like.

Why does the theta beat sound like a hum?

Theta 6 Hz is a binaural beat, which means a slightly different tone plays in each ear. You only perceive the beat on headphones. On speakers the two tones mix into a plain hum.

Can I use my own music?

Yes, if you have the rights to it. Platforms often mute or block videos with copyrighted tracks. The built-in sounds are generated in your browser, so they're safe to use.

Does it remember my settings?

Yes. Your settings are saved in this browser on this device. Files aren't saved, so you'll add your recording again next time.

Exporting

How long does rendering take?

In Chrome, Edge and Safari a 10-minute video usually takes a few minutes. Browsers without a fast video encoder record in real time, so a 10-minute video takes 10 minutes. Choose 720p to speed things up, especially on a phone.

The export stopped or my phone went to sleep.

Keep the studio open and in front while it renders. The page asks your device to keep the screen awake, but switching apps can still pause it. For long videos, use 720p or render on a computer.

Instagram won't accept my video.

Some browsers can only pack the sound as Opus, which Instagram doesn't accept. The studio tells you when this happens. Render the video in Chrome on Android, Edge or Safari to get AAC sound instead.

Can I export just the audio?

Yes. Choose Audio only in the export step to get an audio file of the full mix.

Where does the file go?

Press Save video and your browser downloads it like any other file. On iPhone it goes to the Files app, where you can share it to Photos.
