What's inside

Everything a subliminal needs

Audio mixing, visuals and export in one page. There's nothing to install and no timeline to learn.

A calmer voice

One tap quiets background noise, softens harsh sounds, evens out your level and adds a soft room. You can also stack up to five layers of your voice.

Generated ambience

Rain, ocean, wind, fireplace, a dream pad, pink and brown noise, and a theta binaural beat. You can mix several, and none of them carry a copyright claim.

Precise levels

Set the voice from −45 to 0 dB under the background, with presets from clearly audible to faint.

Fifteen moving scenes

Sparkles, rain, snow, petals, bubbles, fireflies, hearts, bokeh, a moonlit sea, clouds and more, plus a voice orb and waveform that react to your recording, or your own photo.

Your words, in time

With a recording, the studio writes out what you say and shows each line as you say it, in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Without one, it shows the affirmations you type. Every word on the video can be changed or removed.

Private by design

Rendering happens on your device. Your voice, photo and music never leave it.

How it works

Six steps, one screen

  1. Voice

    Add your affirmation recording (audio, or a video with sound). Pick a calm or dreamy tone, how quiet it sits, how many layers and the speed. The video is as long as your recording unless you choose to repeat it.

  2. Sound

    Pick one or more background sounds, or add music you have the rights to. Set the background level and the fades.

  3. Format

    Choose the shape for where you'll post it, at 1080p or 720p.

  4. Look

    Choose a moving scene and colours, or use your own photo.

  5. Text

    Change or remove the title, the small line under it and your handle, or add more lines. With a recording, your spoken words appear on screen in time with your voice; without one, the affirmations you type are shown.

  6. Export

    Preview the real mix, then create the video or audio and save it to your device.
