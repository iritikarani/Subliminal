Privacy

Your files stay yours

In short, Subliminal Studio runs entirely in your browser and never uploads your files.

What happens to your files

The voice recording, music and photo you add are read by your browser and processed on your device. They aren't sent to any server, and no one else can see them. When you close the page they're gone.

What is stored on your device

The studio saves your settings (levels, colours, title, on-screen text and so on) in your browser's local storage so they're there next time. You can clear them at any time by clearing site data for this website in your browser settings. Your files are never stored.

Third parties

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard request information such as IP addresses when pages load. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Your browser requests them from Google's servers. See Google's privacy policy.

There are no analytics, ads, trackers or cookies.

Content you make

The videos you create are yours. Only use recordings, music and images you have the right to use.
